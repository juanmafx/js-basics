# Notice

The exercises in `src/arrays/`, `src/control-flow/`, `src/functions/`, and `src/fundamentals/` are derived from exercises written in 2019 while studying JavaScript fundamentals. Their topics, ordering, and sample data appear to follow the JavaScript Basics material by Mosh Hamedani (Code with Mosh). All rights to that material remain with its author.

The number theory, random number generation, statistics, experiment, CLI, schema, and test code was added during the 2026 modernization.

Published algorithms implemented here:

- xoshiro128\*\* by David Blackman and Sebastiano Vigna
- The MurmurHash3 32-bit finalizer by Austin Appleby, used for seed expansion
- Miller-Rabin primality testing, with the 13-base bound published by Sorenson and Webster
- Fisher-Yates shuffle, in the Durstenfeld form
- Welford's online variance and Neumaier's compensated summation
- The Lanczos approximation of the gamma function
